# The 7-Year Arc: Luke's Mixes (2018–2025)

*Master reference document for liner note generation. Synthesized from 46 analysis reports.*

---

## The Shape of the Journey

### Era 1: The Marathons (Mixes 01–06, Sep 2018 – Dec 2019)

The mixes begin as sprawling sessions — 3h45m, 3h42m, 3h32m for the first four. These aren't sets; they're explorations. Mix 01 alone contains 90 identified tracks across nearly four hours. The instinct is to include everything, to keep the energy going, to never let the moment end.

But even within this first year, the editing begins. Mix 05 drops to 2h39m. Mix 06 lands at 2h07m. The track count falls from 90 to 52. Luke is learning that restraint can say more than abundance.

**Defining characteristics:** Pop crossover comfort (Selena Gomez, Drake, Ed Sheeran alongside Kölsch and Bicep), flat/steady energy arcs, exploratory genre mixing. The foundation artists appear: RÜFÜS DU SOL (every era), Bicep (the gateway drug), Lane 8, Solee, Mood II Swing.

**Energy:** Consistent ~0.23. No dramatic peaks or valleys. The mixing style is more about sustaining a mood than building to a climax.

### Era 2: The Pandemic Mixes (Mixes 07–12, Mar 2020 – Dec 2020)

Sessions tighten to 90–148 minutes. The world shrinks to four walls, and the mixes respond. House gains prominence — deeper, more interior. Techno edges sharpen. The pop crossovers thin out.

Mix 09 (June 2020) is the first sub-90-minute mix at 89m. Mix 12 (December 2020) has the lowest energy of the era (0.211) and the fewest tracks (43) — the moodiest, most atmospheric mix yet. YOTTO & Cassian's "Grains" gets a reported 12+ minute ride. This is someone learning to sit with a track instead of rushing to the next one.

**Key arrivals:** Gorgon City (stays through Mix 40), MEDUZA (through Mix 33), Paperclip People's "Throw" (becomes a signature — 7 mixes total). The "building" energy shape appears frequently — mixes that escalate rather than maintain.

### Era 3: The Refinement (Mixes 13–16, Feb 2021 – Dec 2021)

Four mixes, each distinct. Mix 13 (74m) is the tightest yet. Mix 15 (156m) is a throwback marathon. Mix 16 (127m) shows the first significant energy dip (0.210) — a sign of things to come.

John Summit enters at Mix 14 and becomes a fixture (7 mixes through Mix 45). The ratio of returning artists to new ones increases — the palette is narrowing, becoming more deliberate.

### Era 4: The Consolidation (Mixes 17–23, Jan 2022 – Oct 2022)

Mix 17 (58m) is the first true sub-hour mix. Something has shifted. The mixes are now statements, not explorations. Fred again.. appears in 5 of these 7 mixes — the defining voice of this era.

Energy stays relatively high (~0.23–0.25). The genres consolidate around Dance, House, and Electronic with Techno accents. Pop is present but measured.

Mix 23 (October 2022) is a notable outlier at 116m with 89 tracks — the last big session for a while.

### Era 5: The Quiet Turn (Mixes 24–35, Jan 2023 – Jan 2024)

The most dramatic shift in the entire archive happens here. Between Mix 30 (energy 0.249) and Mix 31 (energy 0.145), the mean energy drops by 42%. It never fully recovers.

Mix lengths settle to 55–112 minutes. The mixes become more introspective, more atmospheric. The energy arcs flatten — not the "steady groove" flatness of the early marathons, but something quieter, more contemplative.

328 new artists appear in this era — the highest exploration rate since the beginning. But the character of the exploration has changed. It's not "everything at once" — it's searching for a specific thing.

### Era 6: The New Identity (Mixes 36–46, Mar 2024 – Jul 2025)

The quiet continues. Mean energy hovers around 0.15 — roughly 65% of the early marathon era. Mix lengths are disciplined: mostly 50–83 minutes.

Then Mix 46 (July 2025) arrives at 103 minutes with 80 tracks and energy back up to 0.202. It reads like a synthesis — the discipline of the recent era with the ambition of the early ones.

Hip-Hop/Rap gains its strongest presence (20 tracks across the era). The genre palette widens again without losing the focus gained through years of editing.

## The Anchor Artists

### The Lifers (present across the entire span)

- **RÜFÜS DU SOL** (14 mixes, 01–46): The emotional anchor of the entire archive. Present in every single era. The one constant.
- **Solee** (8 mixes, 01–46): Deep house foundation. "Shanti" is the thread.
- **Cassian** (span 02–46): Nearly the full journey. Australian electronic connection.
- **deadmau5** (8 mixes, 01–45): Electronic backbone. Progressive house lineage.

### The Era-Definers

- **Bicep** (13 mixes, 01–35): The most important discovery of the early years. "Glue" in Mix 01 sets the tone. Drops off after 2023 — outgrown or graduated from.
- **Fred again..** (11 mixes, 03–45): Modern electronic's voice. Heaviest presence in the 2022 consolidation.
- **Andy Compton** (16 mixes, 02–42): The deep house constant. Highest recurrence of any artist.
- **Lane 8** (8 mixes, 01–36): Melodic house through the first five years.
- **John Summit** (7 mixes, 14–45): The tech-house era. Arrives in 2021, stays through 2025.
- **Gorgon City** (8 mixes, 07–40): UK bass-house. The pandemic discovery that stuck.

### The Bookends

- **Erre – "Always the Same Eyes"**: Mixes 01, 02, 13, 24, and 46. First and last mix.
- **Robin S.**: Mixes 04, 06, 14, 37, and 46. Classic diva house spanning the full arc.
- **Energy 52 – "Café Del Mar"**: Mixes 03–46. The Balearic thread.

## Macro Patterns

### Duration: From Marathon to Statement
- Mixes 01–04: avg 205 minutes (3.4 hours)
- Mixes 05–12: avg 126 minutes (2.1 hours)
- Mixes 13–23: avg 87 minutes (1.5 hours)
- Mixes 24–35: avg 77 minutes (1.3 hours)
- Mixes 36–45: avg 61 minutes (1.0 hour)
- Mix 46: 103 minutes — the return

### Energy: The Quiet Turn
- Mixes 01–30: mean energy ~0.233
- Mixes 31–44: mean energy ~0.158 (32% drop)
- Mixes 45–46: mean energy ~0.197 (partial recovery)

### Genre: The Underground Drift
- Early: Dance dominant, Pop/Alternative crossover common
- Middle: House and Techno gain ground, Pop thins
- Late: Dance/House/Electronic core tightens, Hip-Hop/Rap emerges as new crossover

### Artist Discovery Rate
- 2018–2019: 570 new artists (all new — the big bang)
- 2020: 264 new (76% new)
- 2021: 165 new (69% new)
- 2022: 222 new (66% new)
- 2023: 328 new (67% new)
- 2024–2025: 284 new (70% new)

Discovery rate stays remarkably consistent at ~67-76% new artists per era. Luke never stops exploring — but the context of exploration changes from "everything" to "the right thing."

## The Narrative Thread

This is a DJ learning in public. The early marathons are someone who can't stop, who wants to share everything they've found. The pandemic mixes are someone forced inward, discovering that depth beats breadth. The consolidation is someone who knows their taste. The quiet turn is someone whose relationship with music has matured — less about energy, more about mood. And Mix 46, the most recent, reads like a thesis statement: disciplined length, returning energy, 80 tracks that span the full genre palette.

Seven years. Forty-six chapters. The story of someone learning that saying less can mean more — and then, in the end, finding the confidence to say more again.
