## Liner Notes

A second take of Mix 25, recorded in the same session. The tracklist is nearly identical — the same journey from meditation through techno and deep house to Donna Lewis's closing "I Love You Always Forever," with minor timing shifts in the transitions. Two recordings of the same night, preserved as separate chapters.

The existence of both versions is significant. It's the first evidence in the archive of the DJ treating mixing as a craft that requires multiple passes rather than a spontaneous expression captured once. The selections and the emotional arc are the same. What differs is invisible to the tracklist and audible only in the mix itself: the precise moment where one track bleeds into another, the way a vocal sits against an incoming bass line, the fraction of a second that separates a good transition from a great one. The liner notes for Mix 25 apply in full here — this is the same chapter, told twice, refined through repetition.
